How to Handle Medical Emergency for Parents

A step-by-step emergency plan every family should have ready.

Medical emergencies do not come with a warning. A fall, a sudden chest pain, or a stroke can happen at any time. In those moments, every minute counts. Having a clear plan can be the difference between life and death.

Most Indian families are not prepared for such situations. They panic, lose time searching for phone numbers, and do not know who to call. This article will help you build a simple emergency plan for your parents.

1. Save Emergency Numbers Everywhere

The first step is to have the right numbers saved. Keep these in every family member's phone:

  • Ambulance: 108
  • Police: 100
  • Fire: 101
  • Family Doctor: Name + Number
  • Nearest Hospital: Name + Number + Address
  • Trusted Neighbour: Name + Number
  • Blood Group Info: All family members

Also, print this list and stick it on the fridge. In an emergency, the phone may be lost or locked.

2. Keep a Medical File Ready

Create a simple file (physical and digital) with:

  • List of current medicines with dosage
  • Allergies (if any)
  • Past surgeries and hospitalizations
  • Recent test reports (BP, sugar, ECG)
  • Insurance card copy
  • Doctor's prescriptions

Keep one copy at home and one in the cloud. This is invaluable when you rush to a hospital.

3. Learn Basic First Aid

Every adult in the family should know:

  • CPR: For cardiac arrest. Watch a YouTube tutorial and practice.
  • Choking: Heimlich maneuver.
  • Falls: Do not move the person if you suspect a fracture. Call 108.
  • Stroke (FAST test): Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call 108.

4. Know the Nearest Hospital

Do not wait for an emergency to find out. Know in advance:

  • Which hospital is nearest?
  • Does it have 24-hour emergency?
  • Does it accept your insurance?
  • What is the fastest route at night?

Save the route on Google Maps as "Emergency Hospital".

5. Set Up an Emergency Alert System

If your parents live alone, they need a way to call for help instantly. Options include:

  1. A simple emergency button on their phone (speed dial 1 = you, 2 = neighbour, 3 = doctor)
  2. A medical alert device that can be worn as a pendant
  3. An app with an emergency button like ElderCare Connect which sends an alert to family members instantly

6. Have a Family Emergency Plan

Decide in advance:

  • Who will go to the hospital first?
  • Who will arrange money?
  • Who will inform other family members?
  • Who will stay with the other parent?

Write this down and share it with everyone.

7. Prepare a Go-Bag

Keep a small bag ready with:

  • Medical file
  • Insurance card
  • Small cash (₹5000-10,000)
  • Phone charger and power bank
  • Extra pair of glasses
  • Basic medicines for one day
  • A change of clothes

8. Stay Calm During Emergency

Panic wastes precious seconds. If you practice and prepare, you will act calmly. Remember:

  • Call 108 first if life-threatening
  • Do not move an injured person unnecessarily
  • Note the time symptoms started (important for stroke)
  • Bring the medical file to the hospital

Conclusion

Medical emergencies are unpredictable. But being prepared is in your hands. Save the numbers, keep the file ready, learn basic first aid, and have a plan. Talk to your family about it this week — you never know when it will save a life.
