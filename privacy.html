Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

ElderCare Connect respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and health data. This policy explains how we handle your information in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. Data We Store

All data you enter (senior's name, medicines, vitals, appointments, tasks) is stored only on your device using browser localStorage. We do not have a server that stores your data. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone, or Aadhaar.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your health data for advertising.
  • We do not collect data from children under 18.

3. Cookies

We use a simple cookie to remember your consent choice. We may use Google AdSense in the future, which may use cookies to show relevant ads.

4. Your Rights (DPDP Act)

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data. Since all data is on your device, you can delete it anytime by clearing browser data.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy concern, email us at raisshabbirpathan@gmail.com.
