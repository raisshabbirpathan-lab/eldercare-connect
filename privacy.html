Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

ElderCare Connect respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and health data. This policy explains how we handle your information in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. Data We Store

All data you enter (senior's name, medicines, vitals, appointments, tasks) is stored only on your device using browser localStorage. We do not have a server that stores your data. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone, or Aadhaar.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your health data for advertising.
  • We do not collect data from children under 13. This app is not intended for children.
  • We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to use this app.

3. Children's Privacy (DPDP Act, Section 9)

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, a child is defined as any person below 18 years of age. Our app is not designed for use by children. We do not knowingly collect or process personal data of children. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information, they should clear the browser data on the device.

We do not engage in tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children.

4. Cookies

We use a simple cookie to remember your consent choice. We may use Google AdSense in the future, which may use cookies to show relevant ads. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

5. Your Rights (DPDP Act)

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data. Since all data is on your device, you can delete it anytime by clearing browser data. You can also withdraw consent by clearing cookies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your data. Since data is stored only on your device, the risk of a server-side data breach is eliminated.
