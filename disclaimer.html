Medical Disclaimer

Last Updated: January 2026

This app is for educational and personal tracking purposes only. It does not provide medical advice.

1. Not a Substitute for Doctor

The information and medicine database in this app is for general awareness only. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

2. Always Consult a Doctor

Before taking any medicine, changing dosage, or stopping a prescription, always consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist. Never ignore medical advice because of something you read here.

3. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Using this app does not create a doctor-patient relationship between you and ElderCare Connect.

4. Emergency

In case of a medical emergency, call 108 (India ambulance) or visit the nearest hospital immediately.

5. Not for Children

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used this app, please clear the browser data on the device. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Accuracy

We try our best to keep medicine information accurate, but we do not guarantee it. Always check the actual medicine packet and doctor's prescription.
